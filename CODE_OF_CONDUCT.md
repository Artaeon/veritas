# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Veritas respectful, constructive,
and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give and receive technical feedback with patience and specificity.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, mistakes, limitations, and prior work honestly.
- Keep domain discussions proportionate to the project's experimental status.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention;
- insults, intimidation, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing private information without explicit permission;
- knowingly misleading claims about safety, correctness, certification, or
  suitability for high-impact decisions;
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, code review,
community discussions, and public events where someone is representing the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `raphael.lugmayr@stoicera.com`. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical. The maintainer may remove
content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently
exclude a participant depending on severity and pattern of behavior.

Good-faith disagreement with a technical decision is not itself misconduct.
Enforcement decisions should be based on observable behavior and documented
consistently.
